Requirements:
- An original dialogue between two (occasionally three) named speakers,
  roughly 10-20 turns, that plays out the scenario naturally from greeting to
  a clean ending.
- Format each turn as a Markdown line starting with the speaker's name in
  bold, e.g. **Anna:** followed by the spoken sentence(s). One turn per line,
  with a blank line between turns.
- Write in English. Keep the language natural but learner-friendly: everyday
  vocabulary, complete sentences, no heavy slang or idioms that would defeat
  a learner.
- You may add at most one short italic stage direction line if the scene
  needs it, e.g. *At the counter of a small cafe.*
- No top-level title heading, no [[wiki links]], no footnotes.
- Output only the conversation, nothing else.
