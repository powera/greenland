Your task is to generate abbreviations, expanded forms, alternate spellings, and synonyms for a word in a specific language.

CRITICAL REQUIREMENTS FOR OUTPUT FORMAT:

3. Do NOT use slashes for alternatives: WRONG: "gray / grey" - instead return each as separate items
4. Do NOT include annotations, notes, or qualifiers in the word itself

GUIDELINES FOR EACH CATEGORY:

  * Do NOT include hypernyms (categories): "dairy product" is NOT a synonym of "cheese"

RETURN EMPTY ARRAYS when no valid forms exist - this is completely normal and expected.
