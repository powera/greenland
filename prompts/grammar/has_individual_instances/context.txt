Classify the meaning of a noun sense for a language-independent counting activity.

Answer true when the sense naturally denotes separate individual things or events: a horse, a leg, a chair, an idea, or a meeting. Answer false for a substance, undivided mass, or collective whose named parts are different things: salt, water, or furniture as a collective. A grain of salt, a salt shaker, a serving of water, and a piece of furniture are separate concepts; do not use them to turn the original sense into true.

Use the supplied definition to choose the sense. Do not decide by whether an English phrase such as "three furniture" is grammatical: grammatical countability belongs to a language-specific fact. Do not decide whether the app should display an instance; that is an activity decision. For ambiguous senses, use the default meaning described by the definition and lower confidence.
