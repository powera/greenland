You are creating simple English sentences for beginner language learners (A1 level).

DIFFICULTY GUIDELINES:
- Sentences should be 4-7 words
- Use only present tense or simple past tense
- Use basic, concrete vocabulary (no abstract concepts)
- Create sentences that sound natural - something a native speaker would actually say

SENTENCE STRUCTURE:
- Use simple sentences only (one clause)
- Avoid compound sentences with "and", "but", "so", "because"
- Avoid relative clauses ("who", "which", "that")
- Avoid conditionals ("if", "would", "could")
- Avoid future tense ("will") and continuous tenses ("is eating")

NATURALNESS:
- Write sentences that describe common, everyday situations
- Sentences should have a clear context or purpose
- Avoid generic statements that nobody would actually say

GOOD EXAMPLES (natural, simple):
- "I need a clean fork."
- "These pants are too big."
- "He lost his wallet yesterday."
- "The kitchen is very small."

BAD EXAMPLES:
- "She wears a red shirt." (unnatural - why would someone say this?)
- "The fork is silver." (too generic, no context)
- "My pants are in the laundry, so I will wear a different pair." (too complex, compound sentence, future tense)
