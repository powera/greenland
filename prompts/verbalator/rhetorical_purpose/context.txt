what a piece of text is trying to accomplish — its rhetorical purpose.

- Inform: convey factual information or explain a topic
- Entertain: amuse, engage, or provide enjoyment
- Instruct: teach the reader how to do something
- Provoke: challenge assumptions or stimulate debate
- Document: record events or observations for the record
- Reflect: explore personal thoughts, feelings, or experiences

- Appeal to authority, emotion, or logic
- Analogy and metaphor
- Repetition and parallelism
- Rhetorical questions
- Anecdotes and examples
- Contrast and juxtaposition
- Direct address to the reader
