Analyze text for the following forms of non-literal expression:
- Verbal irony: saying the opposite of what is meant
- Situational irony: a contrast between expectation and reality described in the text
- Hyperbole: extreme exaggeration used for effect
- Understatement: deliberately downplaying something significant
- Deadpan: humorous or sarcastic delivery without obvious markers
- Rhetorical questions: questions asked for effect rather than answers
- Mock sincerity: feigning agreement or praise to make the opposite point
