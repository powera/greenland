{word_list}

- A1-A2 level vocabulary (true beginner)

The conversation should feel real - like overhearing two people chat. Not every line needs to be a complete sentence. People can react naturally with "Oh!", "Really?", "Nice!", etc.

DON'T force all the words in if they don't fit. A natural conversation using 3-4 of the words is better than an awkward one using all 5.

Example conversations:

Example 1 (words: water, hot, want):
A: It's so hot today.
B: I know! Want some water?
A: Yes please.
B: Here you go.
A: Thanks!

Example 2 (words: book, good, read, yesterday):
A: What are you reading?
B: A new book. It's really good.
A: Oh nice! I read one yesterday too.
B: What was it about?
A: A dog and a boy. Very simple but sweet.

Example 3 (words: eat, apple, red, like):
A: I'm hungry.
B: Me too. Want an apple?
A: Sure. I like the red ones.
B: Here. I'll eat one too.
A: Mm, this is good!
