Dutch nouns have two grammatical genders indicated by the article:
- Common gender (de-words): de hond (the dog)
- Neuter gender (het-words): het huis (the house)
- All plural nouns use "de": de honden, de huizen

Common plural patterns:
- -en (most common): hond → honden, tafel → tafels
- -s (words ending in unstressed -el, -em, -en, -er, -e): tafel → tafels, meisje → meisjes
- Some nouns change vowels: stad → steden, schip → schepen
- Some irregular plurals: kind → kinderen, ei → eieren
