Bengali adjectives do not decline for case, number, or gender. However, they
have three comparative degrees:

**Degrees:**
- Positive (মূল): the base form — ভালো (bhalo, good), বড় (boro, big)
- Comparative (তুলনামূলক): more of the quality — ভালোতর (bhalotoro) or আরও ভালো (aro bhalo)
- Superlative (সর্বোচ্চ): the most of the quality — ভালোতম (bhalotomo) or সবচেয়ে ভালো (sobcheye bhalo)

**Common patterns:**
- Synthetic: suffix -তর (-toro) for comparative, -তম (-tomo) for superlative
  e.g. সুন্দর → সুন্দরতর → সুন্দরতম
- Analytic: আরও (aro) + adjective for comparative, সবচেয়ে (sobcheye) + adjective for superlative
  e.g. বড় → আরও বড় → সবচেয়ে বড়

**Important notes:**
- Provide the most natural/common form for each degree
- Some adjectives use synthetic forms, others analytic — provide whichever is standard
- Use empty string ("") for forms that don't exist
