Spanish adjectives agree with the noun they modify in gender and number:

- singular_m: masculine singular (rojo, grande, feliz)
- singular_f: feminine singular (roja, grande, feliz)
- plural_m: masculine plural (rojos, grandes, felices)
- plural_f: feminine plural (rojas, grandes, felices)

Many adjectives do not change for gender (grande, verde, feliz, azul); repeat the same form in both gender slots.

A few adjectives also have a shortened singular used before the noun:

- singular_m_apocope: bueno -> buen (un buen día), malo -> mal, primero -> primer, tercero -> tercer, grande -> gran (un gran hombre)
- singular_f_apocope: only grande -> gran (una gran mujer)

Almost every adjective has no apocope; leave both apocope slots as empty strings ("") unless the adjective really has one.

A prepositional phrase used as an adjective (de madera, a mano, sin azúcar) does not agree; repeat the phrase unchanged in the four agreement slots.

Give forms without articles or nouns. Use empty string ("") for any form that doesn't exist.
