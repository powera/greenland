Maltese nouns have two grammatical genders:
- Masculine: tifel → tfal (boy → children), ktieb → kotba (book → books)
- Feminine: tifla → tfal (girl → children), dar → djar (house → houses)

Maltese has several plural patterns:
- Sound (external) plural: -i, -iet, -ijiet (kelma → kliem, "word → words")
- Broken (internal) plural: vowel changes within the word (ktieb → kotba, "book → books")
- Collective + singulative (siġra → siġar, "tree → trees")
- Some nouns are irregular

Note: Maltese plurals are often unpredictable — broken plurals are common
for Semitic-origin words, while sound plurals are more common for Romance loans.
