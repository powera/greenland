Danish nouns have two grammatical genders:
- Common (en-words): en bil → biler (a car → cars)
- Neuter (et-words): et hus → huse (a house → houses)

Common plural patterns:
- -er (bil → biler, blomst → blomster)
- -e (hus → huse, barn → børn is irregular)
- -r (sko → sko, no change for some)
- Zero plural (et dyr → dyr, no change)
- Some nouns are irregular (barn → børn, mand → mænd)
