Lithuanian adverbs have 3 comparative degrees:

**Degrees of Comparison:**
- Positive (base form - nelyginamasis laipsnis): greitai (quickly), gerai (well)
- Comparative (aukštesnysis laipsnis): greičiau (more quickly), geriau (better)
- Superlative (aukščiausiasis laipsnis): greičiausiai (most quickly), geriausiai (best)

**Formation Rules:**
- Comparative: typically formed with -iau suffix (greitai → greičiau)
- Superlative: typically formed with -iausiai suffix (greitai → greičiausiai)
- Some adverbs have irregular comparative forms
- Stem changes may occur (gražiai → gražiau → gražiausiai)

**Important notes:**
- Not all adverbs have comparative forms (e.g., time/place adverbs like "dabar", "čia")
- Some adverbs use periphrastic constructions (labiau, labiausiai) instead of suffixes
