English nouns have 2 numbers:

**Numbers:**
- Singular
- Plural

**Important notes:**
- Most plurals add -s (cat → cats, dog → dogs)
- Nouns ending in -s, -x, -z, -ch, -sh add -es (box → boxes, church → churches)
- Nouns ending in consonant + y change to -ies (city → cities, but boy → boys)
- Some nouns ending in -f or -fe change to -ves (knife → knives, wolf → wolves)
- Some nouns have irregular plurals (man → men, child → children, tooth → teeth)
- Some nouns are the same in singular and plural (sheep, deer, fish)
- Some nouns only exist in plural form (scissors, pants, glasses)
- Some mass nouns don't have a plural (water, information, furniture)
- Use empty string ("") for forms that don't exist or are not applicable
