Reports

Read-only analysis passes over the dictionary database

Reports answer “what does the data look like right now?” — coverage gaps, vocabulary distribution, term age. They live in src/reports/ and are run from the command line, since several take minutes over the full lemma table and print tables meant to be read in a terminal or redirected to a file. Pass --help to any of them for the full argument list, and --persona or --backend to choose a database.
