Content created here is exported for use in the Trakaido language-learning app.

Explore

Dictionary Entries

Words carry a definition, a part of speech and subtype, and a learner difficulty level. A level of -1 means the word is deliberately excluded from that language's wordlist.

Multilingual Translations

Translations span {{ languages | default(0) }} languages — Lithuanian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, Swahili and the major European languages among them. AI-assisted, with human review.

Forms & Grammar

Each word can carry inflected and derived forms, grammar facts, alternate spellings, and synonym relations to other words.

Sentences & Conversations

Example sentences link to the vocabulary they use, so a sentence's level follows from the words in it.

Audio Pronunciations

Generated speech for words and sentences, reviewed for quality before any recording is cleared for release.

Stable GUIDs

Every word, sentence, phrase, idiom and name has a permanent GUID. Retired GUIDs are never reissued, and GUID lookup reports what replaced them.
